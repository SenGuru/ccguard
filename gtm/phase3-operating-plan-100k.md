## 1. The $100k Thesis — in plain founder language

**A pure self-serve $99-Team model needs ~1,010 paying logos for $100k. A 3-person organic team cannot acquire 1,010 logos in 12 months. So we do not win on logo count — we win on revenue-per-logo.**

The single path that gets closest to $100k: **land teams cheap on the free→$99 door, then drag a fraction up the expansion ladder** — ~3%/mo of the Team base into $299 Growth (the donut), and a handful of the largest free footprints into $15–45k Enterprise. That collapses the logo requirement from ~1,010 to ~480 because each Growth upgrade nearly triples a unit at ~$0 CAC, and 6 Enterprise logos do the revenue work of ~220 Team accounts. **This is exactly how bootstrapped flat-per-team dev-tools (Aikido) reach six figures with no sales team.**

**The 3 levers that matter most, in order of $-leverage:**

1. **The Team→Growth donut ladder (the load-bearing lever).** ~3%/mo of the Team base upgrades $99→$299 (+$200, ~$0 CAC). This single mechanic produces ~42% of M12 MRR and is the difference between $48k and $85k. **It is the most controllable high-leverage lever we own.**
2. **Enterprise inbound (highest $/unit, highest variance).** 6 inbound deals @ ~$2.2k MRR each ≈ $13k MRR, ~16% of M12 — sourced *from inside the free base*, never cold outbound. Each deal ≈ 21 Team accounts. Model it; do not bank the top of the range.
3. **Free→paying-team conversion ramp (1.2%→2.2%).** Drives the entire self-serve base ±35%. Controlled by the invite/rollup UX and a <5-min time-to-paid gate — the single best ROI for engineering hours.

Top-of-funnel (SEO + viral card + launches) is the *fuel*, not the engine. We need ~25k cumulative free signups — organic can realistically supply that. We do **not** try to carry $100k on signup volume; we carry it on ARPA.

**Projected M12: ~$85k MRR (honest band $82–88k). Base case floor $48k. $100–105k is the ceiling, not the forecast.**

## 5. The Expansion & Enterprise Engine — how 3 people manufacture it without a sales team

**The "sales team" is a weekly ranked list, not a person.** Every account emits a PQL feed: dev-count-threshold crossings, donut-teaser clicks, SSO-settings-page visits, audit-export clicks, self-host doc views, company email domain. The founder gets **one weekly ranked list of accounts that already signaled** — that ranked list IS the entire motion. We reach only into demand that raised its hand.

**Rung-by-rung mechanics:**

- **Free → Team ($99):** Trigger = 2nd teammate runs `invite`, Slack-screenshot fatigue, or need for history/alerting. Gate sits on the **team rollup, never the individual scan** — the viral loop never throttles. Conversion ramps 1.2%→2.2% as the invite/rollup loop matures.
- **Team → Growth ($299) — the highest-leverage rung:** Manufactured by **first-class in-product expansion triggers**, not afterthoughts — (a) seat-band overage nudges ("you're at 11/10 devs"), (b) a read-only **donut teaser** showing a blurred company/personal split → "unlock attribution → Growth", (c) "your scan found leaks in 4 repos across 3 teams" multi-team prompt only Growth resolves. Target 20–30% of Team accounts climb within 6 months; ~3%/mo upgrade pace.
- **Growth → Scale ($599) → Enterprise:** The self-serve Scale rung absorbs 30–60 dev orgs so they don't stall. ~2–4% of Growth/Scale accounts are genuinely enterprise-shaped.

**The Enterprise close (no field sales):** The free scanner is *already running on individual laptops inside big orgs* — Q1's company-domain capture surfaces them. A "Talk to founder" CTA appears **only behind enterprise-gated surfaces** (SSO config page, self-host docs, >60-dev workspace), so every call is demand-qualified before F spends a minute. **Light 2-call close** (discovery + security review) → annual contract via Stripe invoicing. Land at $15–25k (SSO+MDM+dashboard for 100–300 devs), expand to $35–45k at renewal with enforcement/compliance/self-host. Founder throughput capped at ~1 deal/month → **realistic 4–8 deals, plan on 6**, back-half loaded (M9–M12). Enterprise Lite (~$999–1,500/mo) catches accounts that need SSO but won't sign a $15k MSA yet.

**Why this works for a bootstrapped team:** target NDR 110–130%. With NDR>100%, the existing base grows revenue at flat logo count — the real engine. The re-activated `ccguard-server`/`ccguard-proxy` crates already exist, so Enterprise is **re-activation, not greenfield** — the only reason one dev can ship it.

## 7. The Honest Landing Zone

**Realistic M12 center of mass: $75–88k MRR.** That is the aggressive-but-honest outcome — meaningfully above the $48k base case, achieved by *expansion mechanics, not invented signups.*

- **Floor (base case):** ~$48k if no launch breakout compounds and the ladder under-fires (only ~13% of Team reaches Growth, 1–2 Enterprise).
- **Plan of record:** **~$85k** (band $82–88k).
- **Ceiling:** $100–105k — requires a **low-probability stack** (jointly ~10–15%): 2.5% conversion **AND** 3% churn **AND** ~10 self-serve-sourced Enterprise deals **AND** two compounding launches simultaneously.

**The gap to $100k is ~$12–18k MRR.** It is denominated in *revenue, not bodies* — organic can't 3x signups, so the gap closes on ARPA, not funnel. **The 3 specific things that close it:**

1. **Enterprise over-delivers: 8–10 deals instead of 6.** Each deal ≈ 21 Team accounts; the swing from 6→10 is ~$9k MRR. The least controllable but highest-leverage lever — worth disproportionate founder attention because it's the only one with $100k-shaped leverage per unit of effort.
2. **Conversion hits 2.5% (vs 2.2%).** Swings the self-serve base +~35%; controlled by the invite/rollup UX and the <5-min time-to-paid gate. The best ROI engineering focus.
3. **A second compounding launch + SEO out-ranks GitGuardian's free ggshield tail**, pushing signups toward the 28–32k top of the organic range and churn to 3% via annual + donut stickiness.

**Honest verdict:** weighting expansion + enterprise pulls the credible outcome from the doc's $30–50k base case to **~$75–88k MRR**. $100k is reachable only if the Enterprise ladder over-delivers AND a launch breakout compounds. **Bank $50k, drive toward $85k, treat $100k as upside contingent on Enterprise — the one lever worth obsessing over.** The named #1 risk remains: if GitGuardian + Anthropic-native commoditize the free door before SEO ranks and the paid moat is in market, even 2.5% conversion has nothing to convert. The mitigation is the whole plan: acquire on the commodity now, bank the un-copyable bundle (cross-tool + donut + card-pay) into Google and paying teams during the 12–20 month window.
