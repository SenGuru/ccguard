FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does it take to deploy?

An afternoon. You generate a managed policy from the console and ship it through your existing MDM (Intune, Jamf, GPO). There are no agent rewrites and no developer friction.

Does it work if vendor telemetry is off?

Yes. The endpoint record is reconstructed from disk, so there's no blind spot to explain — capture doesn't depend on the AI vendor's telemetry being enabled.

Is this surveillance?

No. Claresso governs company-provided AI coding tools on company-managed machines — the same category as Google Vault or M365 Purview. The agent is visible, the record is auditable, and monitoring is disclosed in your policy.

Can we run it in our own cloud?

On the Enterprise plan, yes — Claresso runs entirely inside your tenancy and your data never leaves it.

How are seats counted?

A seat is an engineer with company-provided AI coding access. You're billed per active seat, annually.
