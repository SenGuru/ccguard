Up and running in an afternoon

Deploy it through your MDM. No developer friction.

1

Push the policy

Generate your managed-settings.json from the console and ship it via Intune, Jamf, or your MDM. It's the highest-precedence config — users can't override it.

2

Endpoints enroll & attest

A lightweight, visible agent captures the full record and continuously attests the policy is intact. Drift and tampering surface the moment they happen.

3

Investigate & prove

Search everything, work the findings and on-task review queue, place legal holds, and export the complete record whenever security, audit, or counsel asks.

generate & deploy the managed policy
# generate the Claude Code managed-settings.json for your org
$ claresso gen-policy --org-uuid org-acme-7f3 --otel https://otel.acme.com > managed-settings.json

# ship via MDM → endpoints enroll and attest automatically
$ claresso fleet --status
  ✓ 216 / 218 devices compliant   ·  2 drifted  ·  0 tampered
