Five different ideas (not just type swaps). Each is vector, single-color-safe, and favicon-tested. Accent shown in Azure but swappable. Numbered 07–11 to continue from round 1.

07 · Bracket-C (code)
The “C” as a squared code bracket — instantly reads developer tool while still being the letter C. Native to the audience.
Signals: code, precision, devtool-native. Rejects: soft/consumer.

08 · Prism (light → clarity)
Clarity literally = light resolved through a prism. A beam goes in, clean separated rays come out. Optical, distinctive, not an icon anyone else owns.
Signals: clarity, optics, transformation. Rejects: generic ring/dot marks.

09 · Focus frame (viewfinder)
Camera focus brackets snapping onto a point — “bring it into focus / in frame.” Reads visibility without an eye. Very devtool/precision.
Signals: focus, in-frame, oversight. Rejects: surveillance / eye imagery.
Claresso
favicon = facet only
10 · Clarity facet
Clarity as a cut, clear gemstone — the facet/table of a flawless stone. The most premium, least “tech-startup” route.
Signals: clarity, precision, premium. Rejects: casual, throwaway.

11 · Negative-space tile
The aperture-C knocked out of a solid tile — an app-icon-first identity. Boldest, most “product,” strongest at the smallest sizes.
Signals: product, bold, modern. Rejects: delicate line-mark.
