Security & trust

Built to be defensible.

Claresso monitors company-provided tooling on company-managed machines, using the enterprise controls Anthropic already ships — visible, auditable, and built for the people accountable for risk.

The category

Legitimate governance — not surveillance.

This is the same category as Google Vault, Microsoft 365 Purview eDiscovery, and GitHub audit logs: monitoring the company-owned tooling the company pays for and provides — not general workforce surveillance. Claresso governs one thing, the AI coding tools your organization issues, and it does it in the open.

Data handling

Your data, on your terms.

Metadata-default, content as a tierStart with usage metadata only. Full-content capture is a deliberate, policy-controlled tier you turn on when you decide to.
Secrets & PII stored redactedFindings keep a redacted preview only — the raw secret or PII value is never written to the index.
Works telemetry-offThe endpoint record is reconstructed from disk, so you keep a complete record even when vendor telemetry is disabled.
Configurable retentionSet how long sessions and findings are kept. Retain for compliance, or age out automatically on your schedule.
Self-hosted optionRun Claresso entirely inside your own cloud. Your data never leaves your tenancy.
Encrypted in transit & at restTLS for everything on the wire; encryption at rest for everything stored.
Lines we don't cross

What Claresso will never do.

No keylogging or keystroke-dynamics biometricsWe never record keystrokes or profile how you type.
No webcam, voiceprint, or emotion inferenceNo camera, no microphone, no affect or sentiment scoring of people.
No capture of personal accountsPersonal, non-company accounts are out of scope and never captured.
No covert or hidden agentThe agent is always visible. There is no stealth mode — employees can see it's running.
No selling or sharing your dataYour data is yours. We don't sell it, share it, or train on it.
No reading content outside company AI toolsScope is the company-provided AI coding tools — nothing beyond them.

Enterprise controls

Built for IT, Security, and Compliance.

SSO & SAMLSingle sign-on with Okta or Entra, and SAML for your identity provider.
SCIM provisioningProvision and deprovision users automatically as your directory changes.
Role-based access controlGranular roles so people see only what their job requires.
Audit-log streamingStream activity to Splunk or your SIEM for centralized monitoring.
