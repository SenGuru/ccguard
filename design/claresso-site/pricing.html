FAQ

Questions, answered.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is any employee with a company-provided AI coding tool. If someone uses Claude Code on a company-managed machine, they're a seat — whether they code daily or occasionally.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — 14 days on Team and Business, with no credit card required. You can capture, search, and replay real sessions during the trial to see the full record before you commit.

Can we self-host?

Yes, on Enterprise. Claresso runs entirely inside your own cloud, so your data never leaves your tenancy and stays under your existing controls.

How is our data handled?

Metadata-default: full-content capture is turned on deliberately, per policy. Secrets and PII are redacted before anything is indexed, and Claresso is telemetry-off capable. Read the full security approach.

Does this slow developers down?

No. There are no IDE plugins and no agent rewrites — nothing changes in the developer's workflow. Claresso deploys via your MDM and captures the record from the endpoint.

How long does deployment take?

An afternoon. Generate your managed policy from the console and push it via your MDM — endpoints enroll and attest automatically.
