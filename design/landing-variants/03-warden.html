The compliance platform for AI coding

Everything you need to govern the tools, in one place.

Warden operationalizes the enterprise controls Anthropic already provides — and adds the system of record auditors expect.

Complete record

Every prompt, response, tool call, and diff — captured verbatim and replayable. Works even with vendor telemetry off, so there's no gap to explain.

Findings & eDiscovery

Automatic secret and PII detection on capture, full-text search across the corpus, legal hold, and one-click export of the defensible record.

Enforcement & attestation

Push a managed policy that forces telemetry, locks login to your org, and blocks personal accounts. Every device attests it stays compliant — or it's flagged.

On-task & roles

Score sessions on whether work landed and matched a ticket. Role-aware anomalies route to a review queue — indicators for humans, not automated punishment.
