01 / SEE

A complete account, reconstructed from the work itself.

Tract reads the record Claude Code already leaves on disk and turns it into something you can actually review: every prompt, response, tool call, and diff, replayable session by session. It needs no cooperation from the tool and works even when vendor telemetry is off — so there is no blind spot to account for.

—Verbatim session replay, secrets and PII surfaced and redacted in place.
—Full-text search across the whole corpus; legal hold and one-click export for counsel.
02 / HOLD

Controls that hold, because they're the platform's own.

Tract generates the managed policy Claude Code respects above everything else — forcing telemetry, locking login to your organization, blocking personal accounts — and ships it through your MDM. Then every endpoint quietly attests the policy is intact. When something drifts or is tampered with, you know.

—Highest-precedence config; users can't override it.
—Continuous attestation: compliant, drifted, or tampered — across the fleet.
03 / JUDGE

On-task, judged on what actually shipped.

Not keystrokes. Not screen-watching. Tract scores each session on the signals that matter to the business — did work land in a commit, map to a ticket, survive review — and lets you define what "work" means per repository. Role-aware anomalies become indicators a human reviews, never an automated verdict.

—Per-repo work definitions: "looks unrelated but is related," handled.
—A review queue, not a punishment engine.
